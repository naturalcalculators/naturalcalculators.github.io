Privacy policy

Last updated 3 September 2026. The short version: nothing you type is ever collected, on the website or in the app. The website has no ads. The free Android app shows one banner ad, and that ad involves Google in the ways described below.

The website

No personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking of individuals on this site.

The website does count visits, using Cloudflare Web Analytics. That records which page was opened, roughly which country the request came from, the kind of device, and which site linked you here — all as anonymous totals. It sets no cookies, does no fingerprinting, builds no profile of you, and cannot follow you to other websites. Nobody, including the site's owner, can identify an individual visitor from it.

The Android app

The calculator itself collects nothing. Every expression you type, every stored variable, your history and your display settings stay in the app's own private storage on your phone. None of it is transmitted anywhere, and the maths works identically with the phone in aeroplane mode.

The free version shows a single banner ad at the top of the screen, served by Google AdMob. To choose and deliver an ad, Google's advertising code inside the app receives your device's advertising ID along with general device and network information — device model, operating system version, and approximate location derived from your IP address — and reports whether the ad was shown or tapped. All of this is handled by Google's own software; the calculator's code neither reads nor stores any of it, and none of it is connected to anything you type. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

The app offers a one-time purchase that removes the banner permanently. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play; the app never sees your card or account details. It asks Google Play whether the purchase has been made and keeps only that yes-or-no answer on the device. Once you own it, no ads are requested and Google's advertising code receives nothing further.

What stays on your device

So that your work is still there when you come back, the calculator saves the expression currently on screen, your stored variable values and your display settings in your own browser's local storage (or, in the Android app, in the app's private storage). This never leaves your device, is not readable by other sites, and is removed when you clear your browser data or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The Android app declares internet access and the advertising ID permission. Both are required by the banner ad, and both are granted automatically without prompting you. The app has no access to your files, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location, and asks for no permission that shows a prompt.

Third parties

On the website: Google Fonts supplies the typefaces, so your browser requests those font files from Google's servers, and Cloudflare receives the anonymous visit counts described above. Nothing else on the page contacts anyone.

In the Android app: Google AdMob, for the banner ad, and Google Play Billing, for the ad-removal purchase. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting and no other third party of any kind. Nothing is shared with anyone other than Google, and nothing you type is shared with anybody at all.

Children

The calculator is suitable for all ages, but the Android app is not directed at children and is not part of Google Play's Families programme, because it carries advertising. Nothing you type is collected from anyone, of any age. If you would rather a child used it without ads, the website version has none, and the one-time purchase removes the ad from the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at this same address with a new date at the top.
