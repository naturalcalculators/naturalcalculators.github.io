Vectors

2D and 3D vectors: sums, dot and cross products, lengths, and the angle between two of them in one line.

Setting up a vector

Press MODE 8. Choose which vector (1 VctA, 2 VctB, 3 VctC), then its size: 1 for 3D, 2 for 2D. Type each component and press =; the cursor moves to the next one. ◀ ▶ move back to fix one, and AC leaves with the vector kept.

For the others, SHIFT 5 (VECTOR) 1 (Dim). SHIFT 5 2 (Data) edits one you already have.

Everything else is on the ordinary keys:

  • + and − add and subtract vectors of the same size.
  • A number × a vector scales it; a vector ÷ a number divides it.
  • × between two vectors is the cross product (3D only).
  • SHIFT hyp (Abs) gives a vector's length.

A dot product is a plain number, so it goes into Ans and you can keep calculating with it. A vector answer goes into VctAns instead, and pressing an operator straight after it carries on from VctAns.

The angle comes out in whatever unit you are working in — check the D or R at the top of the display (more on that).

Two quick checks. The cross product is at right angles to both vectors, so VctA·(VctA×VctB) is always 0. And two vectors are perpendicular exactly when their dot product is 0.

When it will not work

  • Adding, subtracting or dotting a 2D vector with a 3D one: Dimension ERROR.
  • A cross product of two 2D vectors: Dimension ERROR — it only exists in 3D.
  • A vector plus a plain number, a number divided by a vector, or sin of a vector: Syntax ERROR.
  • A vector that has not been given a size yet: Dimension ERROR.

Leaving VECTOR mode with MODE clears all three vectors.
